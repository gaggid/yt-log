Privacy policy

Tripfick Studio. A private tool used by its owner only.

Who uses it

Tripfick Studio is a private production tool. It has one user, its owner, who signs in with a password. It has no public accounts and collects nothing from visitors.

What it reads from Google

When the owner connects their YouTube channel, the tool asks for read-only access to that channel's YouTube data and YouTube Analytics (scopes youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly). It reads the channel name, the list of videos with their titles, publish dates and durations, and the numbers for each video: views, watch time, average view duration, likes, comments, shares and subscribers gained.

It cannot upload, edit, delete or comment on anything.

How the data is used

The data is used for one purpose: to show the owner how their own videos and the ideas behind them performed. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising and not used to train any model.

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

The data and the sign-in token are stored in a database on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any other service.

Removing access and data

The owner can press "Disconnect" in the tool, which deletes the saved sign-in and asks Google to revoke it. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Numbers already pulled can be deleted from the tool's database on request of the owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated.
