Tripfick Studio

A private production tool for a single creator's short videos.

Tripfick Studio helps its owner turn ideas into short videos: it keeps a list of video ideas, writes scripts, makes the pictures, voice and video, and prepares the text for upload.

YouTube analytics

The owner can connect their own YouTube channel, read-only, so the tool can show how each idea performed (views, watch time, likes, comments, subscribers gained) and which kinds of ideas work best. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything on the channel.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no public sign-up. Only its owner signs in.
